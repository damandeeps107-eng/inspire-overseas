Our Mission & Story

Inspire Overseas Consultant is a premium immigration and overseas education consultancy based in Dhanaula, Punjab. Founded on the principle that geographical borders shouldn't limit talent or potential, we were established to provide transparent, expert, and highly customized visa consultation services.

We assist students and travelers with career counselling, university admissions, student visas, visitor visas, work permits, IELTS/PTE coaching, documentation, and complete overseas guidance. Our dedicated counsellors stay updated with changing immigration rules to maximize your success.
